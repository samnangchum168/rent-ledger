Rent reminders

Every morning (about 9am Melbourne time) your Netlify site emails each tenant a reminder 3 days before the due date, on the due date, and 3 days after, but only while no payment covering that due date is recorded here. Set each property's due day on the Properties tab. For this to work, the "Period covered" on a payment must include the due date it pays for. Your records are copied to your Netlify site automatically so it can check.
